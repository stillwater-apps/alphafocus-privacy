Timer App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Overview

Timer is a personal productivity timer. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data Collection

Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All timer session data (duration, tags, details, location text) is stored exclusively on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, your timer sessions are synced across your Apple devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Only devices signed into your Apple ID can access this data. We have no access to it.

No Analytics or Tracking

Timer contains no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party code that collects user data.

No Account Required

Timer does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app works entirely offline.

Location

The location field in Timer is a free-text input where you can optionally type a place name. The app does not access your device's GPS, location services, or any geolocation APIs.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time using the "Clear history" button within the app. This removes data from your device and iCloud.

Children's Privacy

Timer does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no age-restricted content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
